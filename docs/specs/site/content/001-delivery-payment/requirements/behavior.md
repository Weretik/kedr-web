# Rules and acceptance scenarios

## Product rules

| ID    | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| ----- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R-001 | `/uk/delivery-and-payment` and `/ru/delivery-and-payment` expose the corresponding complete information page.                                                                                                                                 |
| R-002 | Explain direct order confirmation: cart, phone and order submission, contact by Telegram/Viber/phone, confirmation of availability, actual price and shipping details.                                                                        |
| R-003 | Shipping is within Ukraine, primarily Nova Poshta; dispatch takes 1–3 working days after confirmation **or** payment. This is dispatch, not arrival.                                                                                          |
| R-004 | Buyer pays carrier tariffs unless otherwise agreed. Risks transfer after handover to the transport company.                                                                                                                                   |
| R-005 | After confirmation the seller supplies official IBAN ФОП/ФЛП details; payment is complete when funds reach the seller account. Cashless wording remains sourced.                                                                              |
| R-006 | Cash on delivery is possible at the carrier branch, subject to clarification during confirmation; do not present it as unconditional.                                                                                                         |
| R-007 | Preserve sourced return wording: within 14 days with product appearance and packaging retained.                                                                                                                                               |
| R-008 | Preserve information-only site prices, final price fixed when payment details are provided, and order-phone messenger correspondence as official communication.                                                                               |
| R-009 | Preserve the complete public-offer qualification and link to the current-language `/public-offer`. Contact actions use the configured company phone.                                                                                          |
| R-010 | All factual copy is copied from the Angular delivery dictionary into an independent site dictionary. Do not invent tariffs, arrival dates, free shipping, online payments or pickup conditions. Pickup/address/hours are absent and excluded. |
| R-011 | Follow the accepted concept using the current site typography, colors, primitives, lucide icons, light/dark theme and responsive structure; all substantive terms remain directly readable.                                                   |
| R-012 | Render main content and unique localized metadata on the server, with self canonical, matching alternates and sitemap inclusion. Preserve the existing environment indexing policy and same public URL.                                       |

## Scenarios

| ID     | Given / When / Then                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Test level                                                                            |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| SC-001 | **Given** either supported language, **when** the visitor follows the existing delivery link, **then** the matching page title, introduction and all information sections are present in that language.                                                                                                                | Component/integration; source review of existing navigation                           |
| SC-002 | **Given** the shipping section, **when** the visitor reads its conditions, **then** Ukraine, primarily Nova Poshta, dispatch in 1–3 working days after confirmation or payment, buyer-paid carrier tariffs unless agreed otherwise, and transfer of risk after carrier handover are stated without an arrival promise. | Component, both languages                                                             |
| SC-003 | **Given** payment conditions, **when** the visitor reads how to pay, **then** official IBAN details after confirmation, payment upon crediting the account, and conditional branch cash on delivery are visible.                                                                                                       | Component, both languages                                                             |
| SC-004 | **Given** order instructions, **when** the visitor reads the four steps, **then** cart selection, phone/submission, messenger or phone contact, and availability/price/delivery confirmation appear in order.                                                                                                          | Component, both languages                                                             |
| SC-005 | **Given** related conditions, **when** the visitor reads them, **then** the complete sourced 14-day return, pricing and official-correspondence qualifications remain visible.                                                                                                                                         | Component, both languages                                                             |
